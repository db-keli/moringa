moringa is a fast, distraction-free reading app for mac and iphone that keeps everything in sync without you thinking about it, assisting reading better than the ios books app. open a book on your mac, pick it up on your iphone exactly where you left off. highlight something, add a note, start writing. it's all there on both devices instantly.

the goal is a reading environment that gets out of the way and lets you focus on the text, while being powerful enough to support the way serious readers actually work. no selling of books, no getting slow when number of books increase

## features

- [ ] schedule time to give recaps over a particular section of read
- [x] epub reading on macos and ios
- [x] real-time sync across devices: position, highlights, notes, drafts
- [x] highlighting with colour coding
- [x] notes on highlights
- [x] draft editor for writing while you read
- [ ] pdf support
- [ ] pdf to epub converter
- [ ] linked annotations: tag a highlight and cite it anywhere in your writing
- [ ] scribbling and freehand annotation
- [ ] handoff: start reading on one device, continue on the other without opening the app
- [ ] focus mode: full-screen, no distractions
- [ ] reading statistics and streaks
- [ ] book collections and shelves
- [ ] search across your entire library and all your annotations
